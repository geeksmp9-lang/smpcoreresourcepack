# Source of these textures

Fishing-accessory and -tool textures/models copied from **Confluence: Otherworld**
(same mod as `crafting_station_refs/` — see that folder's SOURCE.md for license details:
LGPL 3.0, https://github.com/Magic-team-jvav/confluence/issues).

Covers: High Test Fishing Line, Tackle Box, Fishing Bobber (+ neon/krypton/argon/xenon/
helium/lava moss variants and a glowing variant), Minecarp (a minecart skin), Bottomless
Water Bucket, Bottomless Honey Bucket, and Fin Wings.

**Not included** (and not available anywhere in this mod): Angler Earrings, Sextant,
Fisherman's Pocket Guide, and Weather Radio. Confluence only has *recipes* referencing
those - the actual items (and their textures) live in a separate mod, `terra_curio`,
which we don't have a copy of.

Staged here as reference/starting points, not finished original assets.
